## Carpet Cleaning

- Slug: `carpet-cleaning`
- Category: Core cleaning

### Summary

### Introduction

Professional carpet cleaning by hot water extraction, using van-mounted industrial-grade equipment and practical methods suited to the carpet, soiling, and condition of the room.

### Overview

At Advanced Carpets & Restoration, we use a van-mounted carpet cleaning machine and industrial-grade equipment so we can provide high-quality work the first time.

Using a van-mounted system means we do not need your power or hot water. By choosing hot water extraction, you can be confident your carpets are visually clean and treated for bacteria, dust mites, and other contaminants.

Whether you need a one-time deep clean or regular maintenance, we help keep carpets in good condition across Cromwell, Alexandra, Wanaka, Clyde, Queenstown, and the greater Central Otago area.

### Detailed sections

#### Hot Water Extraction (Steam Cleaning)

Hot water extraction injects hot water at high pressure, helping loosen embedded dirt, oils, and debris from carpet fibres. The method is especially useful when paired with the right pre-treatment for the carpet and type of soiling.

- Deep cleaning with hot water helps lift embedded soil from carpet fibres.
- The heat can reduce common bacteria, mould spores, dust mites, and allergens.
- Professional-grade temperature and suction help balance cleaning performance with drying time.
- Dry times are usually between 4 and 8 hours, depending on carpet type, pile thickness, airflow, and how much soil needs to be removed.
- High water temperature can help strip greasy soiling from carpets in pubs, restaurants, and other high-use spaces.

#### Common scenarios where steam cleaning helps

- Allergies or asthma, where reducing dust mites and allergens is important.
- Pet households, where odours and pet-related marks need targeted treatment.
- High-traffic areas that look worn because embedded dirt has built up.
- Routine maintenance that helps extend carpet life when combined with regular vacuuming.
- Commercial carpets with grease, oils, or heavy foot traffic.

#### How often to clean carpet

How often you should clean carpet depends on usage, traffic, pets, and the environment. These are practical guidelines that can be tailored to the property.

- Light to moderate traffic: deep clean every 12 to 18 months and vacuum weekly.
- High-traffic areas or households with kids: deep clean every 12 months and vacuum 2 to 3 times per week.
- Homes with pets: deep clean every 6 to 8 months and vacuum 3 to 4 times per week to manage hair and odours.
- Allergy or asthma sufferers: deep clean every 6 to 8 months and vacuum weekly with a high-efficiency filter.

### Overview

Advanced Carpets & Restoration provides professional solar panel cleaning services in the Central Otago and Southern Lakes area, helping homeowners and businesses maintain optimal solar system performance.

Over time, dust, pollen, bird droppings, and environmental debris can significantly reduce solar panel efficiency. In changing weather conditions, regular solar panel cleaning helps support energy output and long-term system performance.

Our solar panel cleaning service uses safe, non-abrasive methods that remove build-up without damaging panels or coatings. We service residential and commercial solar installations and tailor each clean to suit roof access and system layout.

### Detailed sections

#### When to book professional solar panel cleaning

Recommended cleaning is twice a year. One clean at the end of autumn helps maximise power input over the low-sun winter months, and a second clean at the end of spring removes pollen and dust to help keep efficiency as high as possible.

- Dirty panels can lose up to 30% efficiency depending on the situation.
- Residential and commercial arrays can both benefit from regular cleaning.
- Cleaning should be tailored to roof access, system layout, and panel condition.
